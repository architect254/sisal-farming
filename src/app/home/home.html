UNIAO GROUP

Uniao Group Limited is a well-established producer of premium sisal fiber with main production facilities in Nairobi, Kenya.

We own and operate a flourishing sisal business, covering full-cycle operations from sustainable sisal hemp cultivation, manufacturing, spinning, to global export of sisal fiber and sisal products. Our company is well-capitalized, backed by a strong equity base and expert management, ensuring reliable supply of Ug grade sisal fiber, sisal fiber for gypsum, and natural sisal fiber and yarn of Kenyan origin.

Committed to excellence, we deliver SSUG, UG, and polished grades from the best sisal hemp sources. All our products undergo rigorous quality assurance, making us the trusted partner for industries seeking durable, eco-friendly sisal solutions worldwide.
